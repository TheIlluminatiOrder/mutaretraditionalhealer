Lost-love spells

My lost-love spells are intended for people hoping to reconnect with someone who has left or entered another relationship. In my spiritual practice, these rituals symbolize planting seeds of reconciliation, hope, and the possibility of a loving, lasting relationship.

I offer a variety of spells for different people and situations, with space to discuss your hopes and circumstances.

When a former partner has moved on

If you hope to get back together with an ex-lover, you can discuss those feelings during a consultation. This includes situations where the person has moved on to another relationship or is already married.

Love intentions we can discuss

  • Returning a lost lover or reuniting after a separation.
  • Hoping your ex misses you, calls you, or falls in love again.
  • Attracting a crush and welcoming a mutually chosen relationship.
  • Addressing cheating concerns and rebuilding trust.
  • Marriage difficulties, commitment, proposals, and divorce concerns.
